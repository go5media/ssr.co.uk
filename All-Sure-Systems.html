Review overview #

All Sure Systems is a Carshalton-based, family-run provider of electronic security and fire-safety systems for domestic and commercial customers. Our desk-based review found particularly strong publicly reported customer satisfaction, a broad installation and maintenance offering, and published evidence of tailored problem-solving.

Review status: Independent editorial desk assessment. SSR award: Not issued or verified as part of this review. Third-party ratings are reported separately.

Company background #

The business website describes a family-run operation with 20 years of experience in security and fire-safety system design, installation and servicing. Its Checkatrade profile identifies Mr Richard Goodey as owner and lists the business as a sole trader. The website promotes both residential and commercial projects, and Checkatrade reports membership since November 2019.

Business identity caution: We have not established a matching Companies House registration for this sole-trader business. A similarly named company, ALL SURE LTD, appears in the Companies House register with software development as its business activity; we do not attribute that registration to All Sure Systems.

Services reviewed #

Security

Analogue and IP CCTV; intruder alarms; smartphone-enabled viewing and alerts; system upgrades and fault repairs.

Access and entry

Access control, entryphone/intercom systems and gate automation.

Fire safety

Fire alarm systems and fire extinguishers, including installation and servicing services advertised by the business.

Free, no-obligation site surveys are offered, with appointments stated to be arranged around customers’ schedules.

Accreditation and credentials #

The business states that it is SSAIB accredited for intruder alarm systems and CCTV. Checkatrade independently displays SSAIB CCTV Systems and Intruder Alarms accreditation badges. This is meaningful positive evidence, although accreditation scope, expiry and live status should be checked directly with SSAIB before describing the company as currently certified for any specific standard.

Checkatrade also states that the business has passed vetting checks. Checkatrade membership and reviews are not a substitute for technical inspection of individual installations.

Customer service and accessibility #

Customer accessibility is a clear strength. All Sure Systems publishes an office number, a mobile number, an email address and a contact form, and offers free site surveys. Its website says appointments can be arranged to suit the customer. Third-party reviews provide examples of explanations, tidy installations, aftercare and problem resolution.

Contact: 020 8401 1406 · 07817 214238 · enquiries@allsuresystems.co.uk.

Some directories indicate 24-hour operation or call-out, but availability and emergency response terms should be confirmed directly before relying on them.

Technical and bespoke approach #

The website advertises design-led installations and offers both analogue and IP CCTV, smartphone-connected alarm systems and broader integration with entry and gate systems. The breadth of services suggests an ability to address multi-system requirements.

Importantly, the customer account concerning an accessible intercom provides a concrete example of tailoring equipment selection to a specific user need. This supports a positive assessment of practical flexibility. However, we have not inspected technical design files, installation commissioning reports, data-protection procedures or a representative sample of completed systems.

Specific professional CCTV manufacturer competence, cybersecurity configuration standards and long-term maintenance service-level commitments remain unverified for SSR Gold Award purposes.

Website and contact assessment #

The website communicates the main service categories, offers a free survey and provides clear telephone, email and enquiry-form routes. The ability to request an assessment without first selecting a standard package is a positive feature.

Potential improvements include fuller case studies, clearer descriptions of manufacturer partnerships and maintenance arrangements, more detailed technical and compliance information, and explicit accessibility information. These would help prospective customers evaluate complex projects before making contact.

Key strengths #

  • Strong third-party customer feedback across Google and Checkatrade.
  • Broad coverage of CCTV, intruder alarms, access control, fire safety and gate automation.
  • SSAIB accreditation claims supported by listed Checkatrade accreditation badges.
  • Multiple contact channels and free site surveys.
  • Specific published evidence of adapting an intercom installation for accessibility needs.
  • Installation, upgrade, repair and maintenance services rather than installation only.

Limitations and verification #

  • This is a desk-based assessment, not an on-site audit or mystery-shopper exercise.
  • Live SSAIB certificate scope and validity have not been directly checked with the issuing body.
  • No first-hand assessment of workmanship, system cybersecurity, contractual terms or customer support response times.
  • Google review totals are based on screenshots supplied for this review; Checkatrade counts can change.
  • Checkatrade lists a sole trader; do not confuse the business with similarly named limited companies.

Editorial verdict #

All Sure Systems makes a convincing case as a customer-focused security installer. The combination of substantial positive feedback, varied security disciplines, advertised accreditation and specific examples of considerate service is compelling. Its strongest differentiator in the evidence reviewed is not a marketing slogan but the consistency of customer accounts describing responsive, clear and adaptable service.

For homeowners and smaller commercial premises looking for CCTV, alarms or integrated entry systems, All Sure Systems appears worthy of consideration and a detailed quotation. Buyers should still confirm accreditation scope, equipment specifications, support terms and data-handling responsibilities for their particular project.
