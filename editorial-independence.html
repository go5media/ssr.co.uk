Editorial approach Editorial approach

Security Safety Reviewer aims to make assessments useful, evidence-led and understandable. Reviews distinguish observable information, statements made by businesses, third-party feedback and editorial judgement. Readers should consult the evidence and limitations set out in each review.

How reviews are assessed How reviews are assessed

Assessments consider relevant service information, technical detail, customer accessibility, available feedback and documented experience. The weight given to any source depends on its relevance, reliability and specificity. Our review methodology explains the process in more detail.

Managing potential conflicts Managing potential conflicts

Potential conflicts of interest are considered when assessing material. Where a relationship materially affects the interpretation of a particular review, the relevant context should be provided with that review. This policy does not make a representation about the ownership or corporate relationships of the website.

Award decisions #

Bronze, Silver and Gold recognition is considered against the published award criteria. Award identifiers can be checked through the verification process. An award is an editorial recognition, not a regulatory accreditation or technical certification.

Accuracy and attribution #

Claims about qualifications, accreditations, manufacturer relationships, public-safety work and customer ratings should be attributed to their sources where relevant. Unverified statements should not be described as independently established facts. External ratings may change over time.

Corrections and feedback #

Readers and businesses may submit relevant evidence and request correction of a material factual error. Challenges are assessed on their merits, with significant corrections made where warranted. See Corrections, Verification & Contact.

Published: 17 October 2026. These policies describe editorial standards and disclosures; any outstanding operator information is identified explicitly.
