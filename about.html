Who we are #

Security Safety Reviewer.co.uk publishes editorial assessments of security service providers, including CCTV installers and security systems specialists. Its articles consider published company information, customer feedback, accessibility, technical service descriptions and other evidence that is identified within each review.

What we publish #

The site publishes individual company reviews, information about its Bronze, Silver and Gold recognition framework, and award application guidance. A published review is not automatically an award, and a third-party customer rating is not the same as an SSR editorial rating.

Published reviews #

Read our assessments of UK Safety Camera Network Ltd, All Sure Systems and UK CCTV Installations. Each article describes the evidence considered and any limitations that remain.
